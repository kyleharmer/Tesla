What Michigan “REBUILT SALVAGE” actually means
A Michigan rebuilt title is not self-certified.

The state process requires the salvage vehicle to be repaired, TR-13A documentation, repair certification by an appropriately certified mechanic, documentation/receipts for major components, and inspection by a specially trained law-enforcement salvage vehicle inspector. The inspector completes TR-13B if the vehicle passes. Michigan then issues a title permanently branded REBUILT SALVAGE.

This establishes compliance with Michigan's rebuild/title process. It is not the same as Tesla certifying the high-voltage battery, crash structure, warranty eligibility, or Supercharging.

Unsupported Vehicle: what it means
A Tesla declared a total loss or carrying salvage/rebuilt history may be classified by Tesla as an Unsupported Vehicle.

When classified unsupported, Tesla says its limited warranties and extended service agreements are void. The mobile app can still work. Tesla may require a paid High Voltage Safety Inspection before Tesla performs HV-related work. Fast charging is disabled for an unsupported vehicle until it successfully passes Tesla's separate Fast Charging Safety Inspection.

Passing those inspections does not remove Unsupported Vehicle status or restore the warranty.

How authoritative is NMVTIS?

NMVTIS stands for the National Motor Vehicle Title Information System. It is a federally established vehicle-history system overseen by the U.S. Department of Justice. Its purpose is specifically to help prevent title fraud, title washing, stolen-vehicle retitling, and concealment of junk/salvage history.

The DOJ says NMVTIS combines information from sources including state motor-vehicle titling agencies, junk and salvage yards, automobile recyclers, and insurance carriers. Consumer reports can show current title information, state title-brand history such as salvage/rebuilt/flood, latest reported odometer readings, insurance salvage/total-loss determinations, and junk/salvage transfers.

For title brands and state title events, this is much stronger evidence than a dealer listing, free VIN checker, or generic web search. A state brand entered into NMVTIS becomes part of the vehicle's NMVTIS history.
Important limitation — don't oversell the reports:

NMVTIS is authoritative for the records it actually receives, but the DOJ itself says it is intentionally a concise system and is not a complete mechanical, accident, service, or repair history. A missing event does not prove that nothing happened. State practices also differ.

Likewise, ClearVin's “D / Bad” score and EpicVIN's calculated mileage projections are commercial analysis, not government findings. The government-backed facts are the individual records they explicitly identify as NMVTIS-sourced.

My position with the dealer is therefore: “I'm not asking you to accept ClearVin's or EpicVIN's opinion. I'm asking about the underlying Ohio and Michigan title records that both reports identify as NMVTIS records.”

Tesla Unsupported Vehicle policy

Why this vehicle can fall under the policy Tesla's current Unsupported Vehicle Policy says factors include a vehicle being declared a total loss, salvage-titled, rebuilt-titled, or junk-titled. Tesla says it can use government agencies, insurers, Tesla centers, independent vehicle-history agencies, internet forums, and auction sites to determine whether a vehicle has been or should be classified as unsupported. Tesla policy PDF Tesla legal summary
Warranty consequence Tesla states that when a vehicle is classified as unsupported, Tesla limited warranties and extended service agreements are void. Passing later safety inspections does not restore those warranties. Tesla policy PDF
High-voltage service restriction Tesla says only Tesla Service Centers may perform the High Voltage Safety Inspection. If an unsupported vehicle has not passed it, Tesla Service Centers and Tesla Approved Collision Centers may not perform HV-system maintenance/service or the Fast Charging Safety Inspection. After passing, the car still remains an Unsupported Vehicle. Tesla policy PDF Tesla HV inspection authorization
Fast charging / Supercharging restriction Tesla's policy says fast charging through Tesla Superchargers or third-party DC fast chargers remains disabled on an unsupported vehicle until it successfully passes Tesla's Fast Charging Safety Inspection. That inspection must occur at a Tesla Service Center, at customer expense, and cannot begin until the High Voltage Safety Inspection has passed. Even after fast charging is restored, the vehicle remains unsupported. Tesla policy PDF - see Fast Charging section
App access does NOT prove supported status Tesla explicitly says mobile-app usage remains supported for unsupported vehicles. So seeing the car in the Tesla app is useful ownership evidence, but it does not prove the vehicle is fully supported or eligible for HV service / DC fast charging. Tesla policy PDF
Important nuance for the conversation:
Do not tell the dealer “this car definitely cannot Supercharge.” That is too strong before checking the VIN's actual Tesla status.

The accurate statement is: because it is rebuilt/total-loss, Tesla's policy can classify it as Unsupported; if it is unsupported, fast charging is disabled unless the Tesla HV and Fast Charging Safety Inspection process has been successfully completed.

Michigan rebuilt-title process

Michigan rebuilt is a real state recertification process Michigan says a salvage vehicle cannot return to public-road use until it is repaired, passes inspection by a specially trained law-enforcement salvage vehicle inspector, and is retitled. TR-13A is completed with a licensed mechanic; the inspector reviews the salvage title and receipts for major parts and completes TR-13B if it passes. The new title is branded REBUILT SALVAGE. Michigan SOS - Titles Michigan TR-13A
What the state inspection does and does not establish The Michigan inspector checks VIN/parts identification, ownership of repair parts, compliance with Michigan equipment/safety requirements, and that repairs were certified as workmanlike by the appropriate mechanic. That is important evidence, but it is separate from Tesla's HV / fast-charging safety process. Michigan dealer manual - rebuilt salvage
