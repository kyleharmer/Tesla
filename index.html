What “Michigan REBUILT SALVAGE” actually means
Michigan rebuilt status is NOT self-certified.

• A salvage vehicle is repaired.
• TR-13A is completed; repair work is certified by an appropriately certified mechanic.
• Major-component receipts/parts documentation are presented.
• A specially trained law-enforcement Salvage Vehicle Inspector reviews the documents and inspects the vehicle.
• The inspector checks VIN/part identification, ownership of repair parts, Michigan equipment/safety compliance and the certified repair work.
• If it passes, the inspector completes TR-13B Salvage Vehicle Recertification.
• Michigan then issues a title permanently branded REBUILT SALVAGE.

Important: this is a Michigan legal/safety recertification process. It is NOT Tesla certification of the battery, crash structure or HV system.

Tesla Unsupported Vehicle status — know this before asking
A Tesla that has been totaled or carries salvage/rebuilt title history can be classified by Tesla as an Unsupported Vehicle.

If classified Unsupported:
• Tesla limited warranties / extended service agreements are void.
• The Tesla mobile app can still work.
• Tesla may restrict high-voltage service.
• Tesla can require a paid High Voltage Safety Inspection before HV work.
• Only a Tesla Service Center performs Tesla's HV Safety Inspection.
• Passing that inspection does NOT restore warranty or remove Unsupported status.
• Fast charging may separately require Tesla's Fast Charging Safety Inspection.
• Passing a fast-charging inspection can restore DC fast charging, but the title/Unsupported status remains.
Enter Service Mode
  1. Park the vehicle.
  2. Controls → Software.
  3. Press/hold the large MODEL/model-name area for about two seconds.
  4. Release when the access box appears.
  5. Enter service.
  6. Browse only. Don't run resets/calibrations/component tests.
Service History — mileage evidence

  2. Look for date + odometer entries.
  3. Note whether the entry says Tesla, Independent Repairer or Unknown.
